Climate-Resilient Structures: Life-Cycle Performance and Structural Innovation

A Special Issue on structural performance, durability, sustainability, and innovative systems under changing climate conditions.

Climate change is intensifying the environmental demands placed on the built environment, affecting the long-term durability and life-cycle performance of structures. This Special Issue welcomes original research and review articles on climate-resilient structural systems, performance-based design, life-cycle assessment, degradation mechanisms, and structural innovations such as self-centering systems, advanced materials, and novel construction methods.
